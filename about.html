Our Story

An aviation company with a ground team that cares

Airborne Aviation Academy Pvt Ltd was set up to make helicopter and private air travel simpler for families, pilgrims, businesses and hospitals.

Too often, booking a flight meant calling five people and still not knowing who would meet you at the helipad. We changed that with a single flight desk: one team plans the route, arranges the aircraft, sorts the permissions and stays with you until you land.

  • Single point of contact
  • Approved operators only
  • Clear, itemised pricing
  • Ground team at every pad

Mission & Vision

What drives us every day

Our Mission

To offer safe, well-handled and fairly priced air services across India — for faith, for health, for business and for adventure.

Our Vision

A country where a helipad or a medical flight is a phone call away, not a week of paperwork.

Our Promise

Every passenger — pilgrim, patient or first-time flyer — gets the same patient, careful service.

Safety First

No flight goes ahead if weather, aircraft or crew aren't right. We'd rather reschedule than compromise.

Clarity

Itemised quotes, written confirmations and GST invoices for every booking.

Punctuality

Slots, ground transfers and hospital handovers planned to the minute.
