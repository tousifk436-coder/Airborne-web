Air Ambulance

When a patient needs specialist care in another city, distance becomes the biggest risk. An air ambulance can turn a long, tiring road journey into a short, monitored flight.

Airborne's medical desk coordinates every part of the transfer: the aircraft, the medical team, equipment such as ventilators and monitors, and road ambulances at both ends. We stay in touch with the sending and receiving hospitals so the patient moves from one bed to the next without gaps in care.

We arrange fixed-wing air ambulances for long distances and helicopter transfers where runways are far or roads are blocked, and we support international repatriation with the required paperwork.

How it works

  1. Call the medical desk

    Share patient details, current hospital and destination. We respond immediately.

  2. Medical assessment

    Our doctors review the case with the treating team and choose aircraft and crew.

  3. Quote & confirmation

    You receive a clear quote; the flight is confirmed on acceptance.

  4. Bed-to-bed transfer

    Road ambulance to the aircraft, monitored flight, road ambulance to the receiving hospital.

  5. Handover

    The medical team hands over the patient and records to the receiving doctors.

What's included

Included

  • ICU-configured aircraft
  • Doctor and/or paramedic on board
  • Medical equipment and oxygen
  • Road ambulances at both ends (as quoted)
  • Hospital-to-hospital coordination
  • One relative on board where space allows

Not included

  • Hospital bills at either end
  • Special medicines not on standard kit
  • Extra relatives beyond seat limits
  • International visa fees
  • Waiting time beyond the quote
  • Airport charges outside the quote
