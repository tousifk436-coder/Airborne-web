Wedding & Event Services

A helicopter arrival turns a wedding into a once-in-a-lifetime memory. Airborne plans the whole flight — the aircraft, the landing site, permissions, timing and ground safety — so the couple simply steps in and arrives in style.

We fly the groom's baraat entry, the bride's vidaai to her new home, couple joyrides and flower showers over the venue. For fairs, school events, product launches and festivals we add hot air balloon rides, helicopter joyrides and full adventure zones.

Our ground team surveys the venue in advance, prepares a temporary helipad where needed and manages crowd safety on the day, so the celebration stays beautiful and safe.

How it works

  1. Share your event

    Date, venue, guest count and the experience you have in mind.

  2. Venue survey

    Our team checks the landing site, approach and safety area.

  3. Permissions & plan

    We handle the paperwork and plan timings with your event planner.

  4. On the day

    Ground crew sets up, briefs guests and manages safety.

  5. The big moment

    Arrival, flower shower or joyrides — right on cue.

What's included

Included

  • Helicopter and crew
  • Venue and landing-site survey
  • Temporary helipad marking where needed
  • Permission support
  • Ground crew and safety barricading
  • Coordination with your event planner

Not included

  • Flowers for the shower (or supplied at extra cost)
  • Venue decoration
  • Photography and video (optional)
  • Travel of guests
  • Charges by venue or local bodies
  • Weather delays beyond our control
