Air Charter Service

A charter gives you control of the clock. There are no queues, no connections and no fixed timetables. You choose when to leave, where to land and who travels with you.

Airborne arranges helicopter charters for short hops, hill stations and event entries, and private jets for intercity and long-distance business travel. We compare aircraft from approved operators, recommend the best fit for your passengers and baggage, and send a clear quote with flying hours, landing and handling charges listed separately.

Our flight desk handles landing permissions, catering requests, ground transfers and on-site handling, so the trip feels effortless from door to door.

How it works

  1. Share the trip

    Route, dates, passengers and baggage — and any special needs.

  2. Compare aircraft

    We shortlist suitable aircraft from approved operators with clear options.

  3. Confirm & permissions

    Pay to confirm; we secure slots, landing permissions and handling.

  4. Ground transfers

    Cars, catering and lounge arrangements are set up at both ends.

  5. Wheels up

    Arrive shortly before departure and fly. Our desk tracks the flight until you land.

What's included

Included

  • Aircraft with qualified crew
  • Flying hours as quoted
  • Landing and parking charges (as quoted)
  • Ground handling at departure and arrival
  • Flight desk support throughout
  • Standard refreshments on board

Not included

  • Crew night halts not in the quote
  • Special catering on request
  • Ground transport unless added
  • Extra flying from route changes
  • Overtime at closed airfields
  • Taxes not shown in the quote
