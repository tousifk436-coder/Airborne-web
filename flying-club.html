About the club

Where Lucknow comes to fly

The Airborne flying club and helipad sit opposite Shivgarh Resorts at Kankaha, on Raibareli Road.

It's our base for helicopter joyrides, hot air balloon flights, microlight and paramotor sessions, drone flying and adventure events. The helipad has marked touchdown and safety areas, a windsock and a trained ground crew who look after every guest from check-in to landing.

  • Marked helipad & windsock
  • Trained ground crew
  • Certified pilots
  • Visits by prior booking

Visitor Guide

Before you come

Book ahead

Flying depends on weather and crew. Call or WhatsApp to confirm the day's activities and slots.

Carry ID

A government photo ID for every flyer. Children fly with an adult.

Weight checks

Flyers are weighed before boarding — weight limits apply for each activity.

Stay behind the line

Visitors wait in the guest area until the ground crew calls them to board.

Travel light

Leave loose items in your car. Caps, scarves and phones must be secured.

Best times

Early morning and late afternoon offer the calmest air for flying.
