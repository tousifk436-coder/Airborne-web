Passenger Guidelines

Before you board

Valid photo ID

Every passenger carries a government photo ID; pilgrims also carry yatra registration.

Weight checks

Passengers and baggage are weighed before helicopter boarding.

Light baggage

One small soft bag per passenger on helicopter sectors.

Prohibited items

No inflammables, sharp objects or loose items near the rotor area.

Medical fitness

Tell us about pregnancy, heart, lung or mobility conditions in advance.

Children & seniors

Children fly with an adult; seniors get boarding assistance.
