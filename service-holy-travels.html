Holy Travels (Pilgrimage)

Pilgrimage to the Himalayan shrines is a once-in-a-lifetime journey for many families. By road it can mean long drives, steep treks and uncertain weather. By helicopter, the same circuit becomes comfortable, faster and far easier for elderly parents and young children.

Airborne plans the whole yatra: helicopter sectors, helipad transfers, yatra registration support, hotel stays near the shrines and darshan assistance where available. Our ground staff meet you at each helipad, handle weight checks and baggage, and keep you informed if weather changes the schedule.

We work only with DGCA-approved helicopter operators who fly these routes every season, so the crews know the valleys, weather patterns and landing sites well.

How it works

  1. Choose your yatra

    Tell us the shrines, dates and the number of passengers, including any senior citizens.

  2. Registration & booking

    We help with yatra registration where required and confirm helicopter slots and hotels.

  3. Arrive at the base

    Our team meets you at the base town or helipad, completes weight checks and briefs you on the day.

  4. Fly & darshan

    Board with our staff, land near the shrine and complete darshan with assistance where available.

  5. Return safely

    Return sectors are flown in the best weather window, and we hand you over for your onward journey.
