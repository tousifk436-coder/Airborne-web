Aero Sports & Adventure

There is nothing quite like the moment your feet leave the ground. Aero sports let you feel flight directly — the burner's roar under a balloon, the open cockpit of a microlight, the silence of a paramotor wing.

Airborne runs adventure flying at our Shivgarh flying club near Lucknow and at fairs, festivals and private events across Uttar Pradesh and beyond. Every activity starts with a safety briefing and goes ahead only when wind and weather are within safe limits.

We also plan corporate adventure days, school and group outings, wedding entries and festival set-ups — complete with zorbing, climbing walls, helicopter joyrides and balloon night glows.

ExperienceNone needed
CrewsCertified pilots
BaseShivgarh, Lucknow

What we offer

Hot Air Balloon

Tethered and free-flight balloon rides, plus night-glow shows for events and festivals.

Microlight Flying

Open-cockpit trial flights with a licensed pilot over the countryside.

Paramotor

Tandem powered-paraglider flights from open ground — no hill needed.

Drone Flying

Drone demos, aerial photography and beginner sessions within the rules.

Helicopter Joyrides

Short scenic rides from our helipad or your event ground.

Adventure Events

Zorbing, climbing wall and full adventure-fest set-ups with ground crew.

How it works

  1. Pick your adventure

    Choose the activity, location and date that suits your group.

  2. Book your slot

    Confirm with payment; we share the meeting point and what to wear.

  3. Safety briefing

    Your instructor explains the flight, equipment and landing.

  4. Fly

    Take off with your pilot and enjoy the view. Photos and video if you added them.

  5. Certificate & memories

    Collect your flight memories and a first-flight certificate.

What's included

Included

  • Certified pilot or instructor
  • Safety equipment and helmet
  • Safety briefing
  • Flight as per package
  • Basic insurance where provided by operator
  • Coordination on the day

Not included

  • Travel to the flying site
  • Photo and video (optional add-on)
  • Food and stay
  • Re-flights by choice
  • Personal expenses
  • Weather delays beyond our control
