Terms

Good to know before you book

The key terms for all yatra packages. Full terms are shared with your booking confirmation.

Children & infants

Children above 2 years pay the full fare. Infants below 2 years (under 12 kg) fly free with an adult.

Booking & payment

50% advance confirms your seats. The balance is payable before boarding.

Cancellation

A 5% administration charge applies on cancellation; the balance is given as a credit note for future travel.

Rescheduling

Dates can be changed if you inform us at least 15 days before travel, subject to availability.

Weather

If weather stops a Dham visit, a partial refund is given for each Dham missed, as per the terms shared with your booking.

Documents

Carry Aadhaar card or passport for every passenger, including children.

What to carry

Woollens, rain jacket, regular medicines and a small oxygen bottle. Keep baggage within the limit.

Not included

Temple donations, pony and palki charges, and personal expenses.
